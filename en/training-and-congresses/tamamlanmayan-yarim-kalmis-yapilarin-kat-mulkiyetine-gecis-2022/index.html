Converting Unfinished Buildings to Condominium Ownership

Online course 30 March 2022, 18:00–20:00 İstanbul Barosu YouTube ve Instagram

Converting Unfinished Buildings to Condominium Ownership — İstanbul Barosu Genç Avukatlar Merkezi, 30 March 2022. Prof. Dr. Etem Saba Özmen spoke.

Prof. Dr. Etem Saba Özmen spoke at "Converting Unfinished Buildings to Condominium Ownership", organised by İstanbul Barosu Genç Avukatlar Merkezi, on 30 March 2022. The event was held online. The programme also featured Av. Kerem Donat (moderator) and Dr. Ahmet Ayar (opening remarks).
