Consequences of the Annulment of Articles 10(14) and 14 (Final Paragraph) of the Expropriation Law

Online course 21 May 2025, 19:00 Hukukeğitim (online)

Free online course on the consequences of the Constitutional Court annulling Articles 10(14) and 14 (final paragraph) of the Expropriation Law.

The course assessed the effect of annulling the fourteenth paragraph of Article 10 and the final paragraph of Article 14 of Expropriation Law No. 2942 on valuation proceedings and annulment actions.
