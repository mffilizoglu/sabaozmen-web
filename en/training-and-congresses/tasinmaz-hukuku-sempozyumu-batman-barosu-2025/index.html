Real Property Law Symposium — Batman Bar Association

Symposium 1 November 2025, 10:30–17:00 Batman Bar Association, Av. Tahir Elçi Conference Hall

At the Batman Bar real property symposium, Prof. Dr. Etem Saba Özmen gave two sessions: on 'title cancellation' actions and on expropriation law.

Presentations

Actions Wrongly Labelled 'Land Registry Cancellation': the Distinct Types and their Procedural Consequences

Principles of Expropriation Law

Moderated by Av. Baran Ortaboy, the symposium also heard Assoc. Prof. Dr. Müge Ürem Nokay on the consequences of abandoning the 'advance title deed' case law and Judge Dr. Murat Nokay on in-rem actions in the context of competing claims.
