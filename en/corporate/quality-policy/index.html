Quality Policy

Saba Özmen Attorney Partnership undertakes to comply with its Quality Management System and to improve it continuously, on the following principles.

  1. 01

    Client feedback

    Once a matter closes we ask how the process went, and let the criticism shape how we work.

  2. 02

    Competitive quality

    Our measure is the best legal outcome reachable in comparable matters — and the route to it being intelligible to the client.

  3. 03

    Team participation in decisions

    Case strategy is set by the joint assessment of the team working on it, not by one person.

  4. 04

    Continuous research and development

    We follow legislative change and reversals in Court of Cassation case law as a matter of routine, and update how matters are run accordingly.

  5. 05

    Efficient use of energy, and the environment

    Files and correspondence are handled electronically wherever possible, keeping paper use down.

  6. 06

    Training at every level

    Everyone from trainee to partner takes part in regular internal training; knowledge is shared rather than held.

  7. 07

    Continuing academic activity

    Our partners teach at universities and take part in congresses and conferences. That work is listed under Training & Congresses.
