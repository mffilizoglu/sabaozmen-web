Cookie Policy

DRAFT — This text is a skeleton prepared for the partnership's own review and approval; it must be completed by the partnership before publication.

Cookies are small text files placed on your browser by the websites you visit. This policy explains which cookies this site uses, for what purpose, and how you can manage your choice.

1. Cookies we use

This site uses two kinds of cookie:

  • Strictly necessary cookies. Required for the site to work and to remember your choices (language, cookie preference). They cannot be switched off and do not identify you.
  • Optional measurement cookies. These help us understand how pages are used. They are set only with your explicit consent; declining them does not affect your use of the site.

2. Storing your choice

Your selection in the cookie banner is stored in your own browser and remains on your device. It is not transmitted to us.

3. Changing your choice

You may delete or block cookies at any time in your browser settings. Clearing site data resets your cookie preference and the banner will appear again.

4. Third-party cookies

This site uses no advertising network, social media tracker or profiling cookies.

5. Data protection

Where cookies involve the processing of personal data, that processing is governed by our Privacy Notice. For questions, write to sabaozmen@sabaozmen.av.tr.
